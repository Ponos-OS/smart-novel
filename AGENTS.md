## Project Summary

A mono-repo fullstack project for managing and writing novels, reading novels and listening to them.

## Standards & Guidelines

@.github/CONTRIBUTING.md

- Comments only when necessary, this includes docstrings/jsdocs; don't add one just to restate what the name/code already say.
- Be concise, short README, no emojis.
- No extra feature, focus on what has been asked.
- Write in chunks to not overload your write tool!

## Development Process

Each feature is built incrementally against a `REQUIREMENTS.md` at the repo root, split into steps meant to be implemented, tested, and committed one at a time — see `.github/docs/process/PROCESS.md` for the per-step loop and `.github/docs/process/SELF_IMPROVE.md` for the required wrap-up. `REQUIREMENTS.md` and the per-feature self-improvement log are archived (e.g. to a GitHub issue) and removed once the feature ships; a new `REQUIREMENTS.md` is written per feature.
