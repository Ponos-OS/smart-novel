### Step 8c-4, after the user's review

- Deleting a chapter or a novel removes the audio inside the transaction, last, so a bucket that fails rolls the delete back and the writer can call it again. I had put it after the commit with a log line, copying the rule for the cover; the user pointed out that without a TTL on the objects a leftover is never cleaned. Lesson: "log and continue" is for a side effect that is nice to have; when nothing else would ever clean up, it is part of the write.
- The user's questions on the subscription showed a design I had ported without asking why: NestJS published every Beatrice update to a Redis channel that every replica handled, and I kept it. With every replica doing the work they raced on the job key (the replica that released it first made the others refuse to publish READY) and wrote the same row repeatedly.
